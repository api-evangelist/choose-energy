# Choose Energy

> Choose Energy is a free online energy marketplace, founded in 2008 and operated by Red Ventures, that helps consumers and businesses in deregulated U.S. energy markets compare electricity and natural gas plans, view transparent all-inclusive pricing, and switch providers online in minutes. It is a consumer-facing web marketplace and does not publish a public developer API.

## About

- [About Choose Energy](https://www.chooseenergy.com/about-us/): Company overview, mission, and deregulated-market coverage
- [Blog](https://www.chooseenergy.com/blog/): Energy rates, savings guides, and market education
